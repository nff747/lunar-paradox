## Services agreement — clauses to finalize with counsel

- **Parties and scope:** Identify legal parties and governing law; an agreed SOW defines work. Establish precedence where documents conflict.
- **Fees and payment:** Refer to the SOW; clarify taxes, invoicing, late-payment treatment and expenses. No advance amount or non-refundable fee is assumed.
- **Acceptance and changes:** Set a fair review process, defect correction and written change-control procedure. Do not assume silence necessarily waives legal rights.
- **Background IP:** Each party retains pre-existing IP. Identify provider materials and specify the rights the client needs to use, maintain and modify delivered work.
- **New IP:** State whether and when deliverable rights transfer, any condition on payment, and precisely what reusable newly created tooling is retained. No confidentiality or client IP may be published without authorization. Third-party/open-source licences continue to apply.
- **Confidentiality:** Define protected information, permitted purpose and recipients, standard exclusions, security measures, permitted disclosures, duration and return/deletion. Do not promise absolute security.
- **Cancellation:** Provide a written notice process and fair settlement for work performed, agreed non-cancellable expenses, deliverables, refunds and access. Review enforceability and applicable consumer rules if relevant.
- **Liability and warranties:** Define proportionate limits and exclusions with counsel, mandatory legal exceptions, remedies and agreed support; do not promise an outcome or device compatibility that has not been scoped.
- **Data protection:** Identify each party's role; add a separate processing agreement where needed, including instructions, subprocessors, transfer safeguards, incident handling and deletion.
- **Disputes and signatures:** Specify escalation, jurisdiction and authorized signatories. Ensure execution matches the actual provider identity.

## Before first use

Confirm legal operator and address, immigration and tax position, whether the client is a business or consumer, payment and tax treatment, IP chain of title, insurance needs, cross-border terms and solicitor review. Do not send unverified claims or request confidential assets before appropriate terms and secure channels are in place.
