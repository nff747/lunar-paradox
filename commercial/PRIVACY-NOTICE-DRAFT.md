Do not publish the existing page's unsupported absolute promises in its place.

## Browser tools

Explain separately for each tool what inputs are handled in the browser, which assets or requests are sent to the server, whether any saved settings or drafts persist, and whether offline use actually works. Verify with source inspection and network tests before making a no-upload or offline claim. Browser-based processing does not by itself establish an air gap or guarantee security.

## Information received through the platform

When a person submits an application, brief or feedback, the platform may receive the fields they choose to submit, including contact details and project information. Describe actual fields, purposes and optional publication consent only after verifying production behavior. Explain access credentials and local draft storage without disclosing security-sensitive implementation details. Do not promise deletion in every case; requests are subject to applicable rights, exemptions and retention duties.

Administrative access and operation may generate session, security and audit records. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process request metadata such as IP addresses and security logs. Verify which records are actually collected and where.

## Purpose, legal bases and recipients

For each category, confirm its purpose and lawful basis (for example, responding to a request, steps towards a contract, legitimate interests or consent, where actually applicable). List the production hosting, database, authentication, email, analytics, backup and other providers actually used; describe their roles, relevant data and transfer destinations/safeguards. Do not assert a provider or transfer mechanism from development configuration alone.

## Storage, retention and rights

The local development version adds an optional tool-usage toggle for PBR Synth and Optim Compressor. It is off by default, stores the choice in localStorage, and sends event categories and optional helpful/not-helpful feedback only while enabled. Requests still reach infrastructure that may process network metadata for security. Confirm actual production behavior, applicable lawful bases, locations, retention and the right to withdraw before including this description in a public notice.

Describe necessary cookies and browser storage by purpose, lifetime and available choices. Assess electronic privacy regulations and applicable rules for other visitors. State approved retention periods or meaningful criteria for applications, correspondence, reviews, logs, sessions and backups; no durations have yet been approved. Document deletion and backup handling.

Explain access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent where applicable; how to request them; and the right to lodge a complaint with your competent data protection supervisory authority. Do not promise that every right applies without qualification.
