LUNAR PARADOX — Ouroboros Astrolabe — interactive spatial environment

Three.js r170 — MIT license, included at vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt.
https://threejs.org/

Lunar surface color: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit,
2019 LROC color map, 4096 x 2048. Converted from TIFF to JPEG for runtime use.
Lunar elevation: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit,
LOLA elevation map, 1024 x 512.
Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio; Ernie Wright (USRA),
Noah Petro (NASA/GSFC). Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/

The scenes are artistic simulations. HUD coordinates, waveforms and radar
represent scene state, not live astronomical instruments. Frame rates are
measured by the rendering loop and vary by device and browser.

User-supplied reference screenshots guide composition; they are not used
as full-frame backgrounds or substitutes for the live 3D scene.

Barlow Condensed and Space Grotesk — SIL Open Font License.
Local font files and license texts are included under assets/fonts/.

Additional height detail: NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit LOLA ldem_16_uint.tif,
5760 x 2880; normalized to a 4096 x 2048 local height texture.
Procedural crater and fracture atlases are generated data, not source photos.

Fine stone normal detail: Poly Haven, Rock 05, by Rob Tuytel.
CC0 1.0. Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_05
The photogrammetry normal map only modulates surface normals on the live
3D lunar mesh; it is not a scene background. NASA lunar albedo is retained.
