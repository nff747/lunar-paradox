About the library.

High-performance spatial UI engine that replaces traditional heavy DOM elements with GPU-rendered glassmorphic surfaces, depth refraction, and real-time frosted blur shaders. Synchronizes with a screen-reader-accessible semantic layer maintaining high-contrast readability standards.

Setup, compatibility, current releases and the licence are maintained in the repository. Check those before integrating the library into your project.
