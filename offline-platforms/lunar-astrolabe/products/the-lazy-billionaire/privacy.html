Privacy Policy & Account Deletion

This document outlines the privacy commitments, data collection policies, and account deletion procedures for The Lazy Billionaire: Idle Trading Tycoon, published by Lunar Paradox.

1. Data Collection & Usage

The Lazy Billionaire is designed primarily as an offline-first single-player simulation game. We collect only minimal telemetry and save state data necessary for optional multiplayer leaderboards and cloud sync:

  • Guest Mode: In Guest Mode, 100% of game progression (money, assets, portfolio, rank) is saved solely within your local device's encrypted IndexedDB / LocalStorage storage. No personal data is sent to our servers.
  • Cloud Accounts (Optional): If you link Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication, we store your anonymous User ID, display name, avatar choice, and game save state (total net worth, diamond balance) on Google Cloud Firestore.
  • In-App Purchases: Real-money transactions for diamond bundles are processed directly through Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or billing details.

2. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In full compliance with Google Play's User Data policy on Account Deletion, players have complete control to permanently delete their account and all associated cloud data at any time.

Option A: Instant In-App Deletion

1. Open the game on your device.
2. Tap the Status / Profile tab in the bottom navigation.
3. Tap Settings / Disconnect → tap Reset Data / Delete Cloud Account.
4. Confirm deletion. Your Firestore document, save hashes, leaderboard rank, and local save files are immediately and permanently erased.

Option B: Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or wish to request manual cloud data erasure, send an email to:

privacy@lunarparadox.com with the subject [The Lazy Billionaire Data Deletion Request] and include your in-game User ID (or associated Google account email).

All requests are processed and confirmed within 7 business days, resulting in complete removal from Google Cloud Firestore backups.

3. Children's Privacy

Our game does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. All gameplay mechanisms utilize fictional currencies.
