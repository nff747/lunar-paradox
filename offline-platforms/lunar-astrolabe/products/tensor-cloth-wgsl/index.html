About the library.

GPU-accelerated physics engine executing non-linear cloth strain, bending constraints, and self-collision kernels directly inside compute shaders. Runs complex textile draping and aerodynamic wind response at a locked 60 FPS without touching CPU physics threads.

Setup, compatibility, current releases and the licence are maintained in the repository. Check those before integrating the library into your project.
