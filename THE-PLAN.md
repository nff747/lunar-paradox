## 4. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS (TRACK B)

### 4.1 Bounded Cinematic Interaction State Machine
Unrestricted orbital controls, arbitrary pan, and free zoom are explicitly prohibited:
* **The HOLD Trigger:** Press-and-hold charges the kinetic stress field, triggering surface micro-fissures and tension audio.
* **The LATCHED OPEN Corridor:** Upon 100% rupture, the camera transitions to an **authored cinematic inspection spline**:
  - Motion is locked to a predetermined camera spline with clamped translation progress.
  - Damped pointer parallax ($\le 5^\circ$ angular deflection).
  - Pre-selected optical inspection nodes (core chasm, primary fault edge, drifting slab).
  - Fixed, authored Field of View ($42^\circ$ locked, zero arbitrary FOV distortion).
* **The REJOIN Action:**
  - Primary: Persistent on-screen `[ REJOIN FRACTURE ]` button.
  - Secondary: Accessible keyboard shortcut (`Space` or `R`).
  - Redundant: Optional double-click on empty space.

### 4.2 Hierarchical Fracture Topology
The fracture is an engineering problem of geometry, not merely vertex noise:
1. **Asymmetric Primary Bodies:** The sphere splits into two non-identical, non-hemispherical tectonic masses with offset centers of gravity.
2. **Independent Pivots & Angular Velocities:** Bodies rotate around non-coincident, non-parallel virtual axes with staggered acceleration curves.
3. **Detachable Perimeter Slabs:** 4 to 8 distinct geometric slabs detach from fracture edges, vacating the parent silhouette with secondary tumble vectors.
4. **Locally Emitted Micro-Fragments:** GPU-instanced crystalline debris emitted from the active cleavage boundary during the first 600 ms.
5. **Interior Fracture-Wall Geometry:** Watertight geometry capping the rift interior with jagged mineral faceting, exposing crystalline structure rather than hollow backfaces.
6. **Shader Role Limitation:** Shaders are confined to high-frequency normal perturbation, emission/heat luminance along fresh break seams, and subtle micro-relief.

> **Production Scope Boundary:** The Hour-48 sequence utilizes a **Capture Fracture Rig** optimized specifically for the camera frustum of the authored 12–15 second shot. The generalized, multi-angle production fracture system is engineered in subsequent sprints. The public reel must not claim the shot is an omnidirectional, arbitrary-angle solver.

## 5. PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT & THE 6 RELEASE GATES

### 5.1 Three Isolated Measurement Standards
1. **The Public Capture:** Recorded deterministically (frame-by-frame) to ensure zero encoder-induced drops in the video asset.
2. **Warm Live Run (Named Reference Hardware):**
   - Evaluated over a fixed 60-second continuous run at native $1920 \times 1080$, DPR 1.0.
   - P95 frame interval must satisfy $16.67\text{ ms} \pm 1.5\text{ ms}$ cadence tolerance.
   - Outliers $> 33.3\text{ ms}$ are counted and investigated with stack traces.
   - Logger automatically excludes loading screens, hidden tab states, deliberate user pauses, and warm-up frames.
3. **GPU Profiling:** Separate JavaScript `requestAnimationFrame` deltas from GPU draw time using `EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2` where supported.

### 5.2 The 8-Tier Graphical Quality Ladder
1. Dynamic render scaling ($1.0 \rightarrow 0.85 \rightarrow 0.75$).
2. Reflection texture resolution ($1024 \rightarrow 512$) and update frequency (alternate frames).
3. Ejecta/debris particle count reduction (240 $\rightarrow$ 60).
4. Deactivate secondary volumetric dust/smoke passes.
5. Reduce shadow-map cascade resolution.
6. Swap distant perimeter slabs to low-poly LOD proxies.
7. Bypass peripheral post-processing passes (lens streak, grain).
8. **Inviolate Core:** Primary silhouette, fracture timing, and typography remain invariant across all tiers.

### 5.3 The 6 Separable Objective Release Gates (Hour 48)
