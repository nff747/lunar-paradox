## 4. Maker's First Comment (Post immediately upon launch)

Copy and paste this into the discussion section as the first comment right after publishing:

```markdown
Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I'm the creator of **Lunar Paradox** (https://lunarparadox.com).

Modern 3D web tooling often requires bloated desktop electron apps or uploads proprietary 3D assets to remote SaaS servers. We built Lunar Paradox with a different philosophy: **Zero-Server, Zero-Telemetry, Bare-Metal Browser Performance.**

### What's inside the platform:

1. **The Studio** (https://lunarparadox.com)
   - Real-time procedural 3D interactive graphics runtime engineered with raw Three.js and custom GLSL shaders.
   - Locked 60 FPS baseline verified on reference mobile/desktop hardware.

2. **Astrolabe Products & Suite** (https://products.lunarparadox.com)
   - **PBR Synth**: Synthesize normal, roughness, height, and ambient occlusion maps directly in-browser using WebGL framebuffers.
   - **LUT Color Engine**: Real-time 3D Look-Up Table preview and color grading.
   - **HDR Visualizer**: High-dynamic-range environment map probe inspector.
   - **CSS Glass & Glitch Generators**: Generate zero-dependency CSS/WebGL shaders on the fly.
   - **100% Client-Side**: No uploads, no accounts, no server latency. Your textures never leave your GPU.

3. **Gravity Labs** (https://labs.lunarparadox.com)
   - Hydrodynamic particle mechanics sandbox stress-testing spatial partitioning and compute pipelines.

### Open Source & Architecture
All engines are built on top of open-source architectures (`nff747` on GitHub) with Apache 2.0 licensing.

We'd love your feedback on the real-time frame rates and tool accuracy! Try dropping a texture into PBR Synth and let us know what shader pipelines you'd like to see next. 🚀
```
