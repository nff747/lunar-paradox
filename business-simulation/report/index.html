Critical limitations

Founder limited to 160 hours/month, including sales, supervision and R&D. Interns are not assumed to be free senior delivery capacity. Clients, accepted prices, conversion rates, effort and starting cash are unverified. Required funding assumes access to enough cash to finish commitments; the model does not simulate bankruptcy or raising investment. No actual website pricing was changed.

Balanced score: 0.60 × median surplus + 0.40 × p10 surplus − 0.75 × p90 funding need + 0.10 × median Labs spending. Weights express judgement. Alternate ranking priorities show how the decision changes.

These are 10 commercial models crossed with 10 operating plans—not 100 independently validated market opportunities.
