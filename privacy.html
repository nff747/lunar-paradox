Privacy at Lunar Paradox

This page describes how the tools and project application work, and how to contact us about your information. Our formal privacy review is still in progress.

Using the tools

PBR Synth, Optim Compressor, Resonance DSP and Cipher Workbench are designed to process the files you choose in your browser. The website and its code still need to be delivered to your device over the network. Files you export are saved wherever you choose on your device.

Browser storage may keep settings and preferences between visits. Clearing site data removes those local settings. Browser extensions, your device and any files you choose to share are outside our control.

Project applications

When you submit an application, you provide your name, email address and information about your project. The application is sent to the service and stored for review. Please do not include confidential material you are not ready to share.

The form temporarily saves a draft in your browser session. If you choose to save a draft, it is stored on your device with a seven-day expiry checked when the form is next opened. An application access key may also be held in browser session storage.

We use the information you submit to review and respond to your enquiry. No fixed retention period has yet been configured for applications and related records; contact us to ask about your information or request its removal.

Optional product feedback

Some tools offer optional usage feedback. When enabled, this sends limited events such as the tool and action; an optional helpfulness response may also be sent. The feedback events are designed not to include the contents of your files. Your choice can be stored in your browser.

Product reviews

If you submit a product review, we receive the name, email, review text and product version you provide, together with your permission for publication. Reviews are held for moderation; an approved review can display your name, text and version publicly. Your email address is not displayed with the review.

Service providers and security

Hosting and database providers, including Vercel and Supabase where configured, process information needed to deliver and operate the service. Requests may include technical information such as an IP address. Storage locations and provider arrangements are being reviewed.

Your information and contact

Depending on your circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, or object to or restrict its processing. Contact hello@lunarparadox.com with a request or question. People in the UK can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office.

The identity of the data controller, applicable lawful bases, retention periods and international transfer details are being finalised. This notice is not yet a complete privacy policy.

For security reports, see our security contact.
