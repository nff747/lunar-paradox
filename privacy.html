Lunar Paradox ARCHITECTURE REVISION: OCTOBER 2026

Privacy & Architectural Integrity

Lunar Paradox is engineered as an applied spatial computing laboratory and decentralized graphics initiative. We uphold strict architectural sovereignty: compute belongs to the user, tracking networks are fundamentally barred, and intentional project inquiries are handled with absolute cryptographic isolation and minimal retention.

1. Zero-Knowledge On-Device Compute

All client tools hosted on our platform—including PBR Synth, Optim Compressor, Resonance DSP, and Cipher Workbench—execute with mathematical finality inside your device's browser memory (V8/JavaScript, WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, WebGL2/WebGPU, and standard WebCrypto APIs).

  • Zero Payload Ingestion: Images, audio stems, 3D meshes, and binary files loaded into tools are processed in volatile client RAM and are never streamed, cached, or transmitted to any remote network.
  • Cryptographic Isolation: Symmetric key derivation, AES-GCM operations, and entropy harvesting execute through standard hardware CSPRNG primitives. Your encryption keys never touch network packets.
  • Air-Gap Capable: The entire toolchain functions completely offline without internet connectivity.

2. Browser Storage & Zero Advertising Networks

We operate zero marketing trackers, behavioral pixels, fingerprinting scripts, or third-party ad exchanges. Your attention and navigation history are never monetized.

Browser storage mechanisms are strictly technical and ephemeral:

  • localStorage is utilized solely to preserve client-side parameters you explicitly control (such as tool presets, audio monitoring volume, camera viewpoints, and draft project briefs). This data remains strictly local to your machine.
  • sessionStorage holds transient access credentials for active project inquiries, cleared upon closing the session.
  • We set no commercial tracking cookies. Authenticated administrative consoles enforce security via scoped, hardened HTTP-only authentication tokens (__Host- prefixed).

3. Voluntary Communication & Project Inquiries

When you voluntarily initiate a studio commission, submit a project brief, or provide peer feedback:

  • Direct Purpose: We record the supplied contact details (name, email) and project scope exclusively to evaluate engineering feasibility, calculate architectural estimates, and maintain direct technical dialogue.
  • Cryptographic Access: Project inquiries receive a unique identifier and a high-entropy private access key. Only a secure SHA-256 hash of your credential is held on our secure database infrastructure; raw keys are never stored.
  • Zero Third-Party Dissemination: Inquiries are never sold, syndicated, or shared with external data brokers or automated marketing networks.
  • Right of Erasure: You retain complete control over your communications. An inquiry, brief, or feedback record can be permanently expunged upon verified request.

4. Infrastructure & Origin Verification

Lunar Paradox enforces rigorous origin controls. All assets, stylesheets, fonts, and WebGL modules are served directly from canonical infrastructure with locked Content Security Policies (CSP) and Subresource Integrity (SRI) hashes. Connections require verified Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
