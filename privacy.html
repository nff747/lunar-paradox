Zero-Server Privacy Policy

Lunar Paradox operates under a sovereign, privacy-first technical architecture. Our interactive 3D simulations, WebGL runtimes, and zero-server tools execute 100% locally on your hardware. We do not track you, we do not set tracking cookies, and we do not store your data.

1. Zero Telemetry & On-Device Processing

All client tools hosted on our platform—including PBR Synth, Optim Compressor, Resonance DSP, and Cipher Workbench—execute entirely within your device's browser memory (V8/JavaScript, WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, WebGL, and WebCrypto).

  • Zero image, audio, or document data is uploaded to remote servers.
  • Cryptographic key generation and symmetric encryption occur strictly within standard hardware CSPRNG and WebCrypto isolation.
  • All tools are air-gap capable and function completely offline without internet connectivity.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use advertising, marketing, or third-party tracking cookies.

Our applications utilize the browser's native localStorage API exclusively for maintaining client-side application state (such as UI preferences, audio volume level, draft review submissions, and camera perspective presets). This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

3. Third-Party Dependencies & CDNs

Lunar Paradox adheres to a strict self-hosting standard. All scripts, fonts, stylesheets, and Three.js 3D libraries are served directly from our origin domain with locked Subresource Integrity (SRI) hashes and strict Content Security Policies (CSP). We do not load remote analytics trackers, Google Tag Manager, Meta pixels, or external ad networks.

4. Communication & Commission Inquiries

If you voluntarily apply for a studio commission or contact us via email, we use the information you provide solely to evaluate technical fit, scope deliverables, and respond to your inquiry. We never sell, lease, or distribute applicant information to third parties.
