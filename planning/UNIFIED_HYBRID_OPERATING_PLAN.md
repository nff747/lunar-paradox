## 3. The 5-Day WebGL Hardening Sprint (Productized Offer)

### Target Client
Agencies, 3D studios, and funded startups whose WebGL / Three.js scenes stutter at 15–25 FPS or crash on mobile.

### Scope & Deliverables
1. **Draw-Call Batching & Instancing**: Consolidate redundant materials and geometry.
2. **Asset Compression Pass**: Run textures through in-house perceptual compression and pack ORM channels.
3. **Adaptive Resolution & LOD**: Integrate dynamic resolution throttling based on measured frame intervals.
4. **Verification Flamegraph**: Deliver before/after frame-time histograms guaranteeing locked 60 FPS on agreed target devices.

### Commercial Terms
* **Price**: **\$4,500 USD** fixed-fee.
* **Payment**: 50% (\$2,250) upfront before work starts; 50% (\$2,250) upon verified 60 FPS delivery.
* **Input Freeze**: Client assets and repository branch locked on Day 1. No mid-sprint scope changes.
* **Turnaround**: Exactly 5 business days.
